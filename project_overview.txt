Challenge Identification

Challenge                              Mitigation

1. Hallucination / unsupported advice  RAG, source citations, constrained output schema, human approval
2. Poor image quality / lighting       Image quality gate, normalization, augmentation and confidence threshold
3. Domain-specific terminology         Plant glossary, fine-tuned prompts and retrieval from approved documents
4. Changing process constraints        Machine-capability rules, current master data and versioned documents
5. False positives / negatives         Benchmark images, calibrated thresholds and human review for borderline cases
6. Data privacy & IP                   Access control, redaction, private deployment and logging policies
